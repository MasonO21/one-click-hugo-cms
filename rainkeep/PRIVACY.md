# Rainkeep privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Rainkeep ("the game", "we") handles. In short: your game is saved on your own device. When you play online with other people, the game shares a small public profile of your keep and the messages you choose to send; during a playtest it also sends a gameplay report to the people running the test. Nothing is used for advertising.

## What stays on your device

- **Your saved game.** Your keep, heroes, progress and settings are stored on your device (in the app's local storage). We never receive a copy.
- **Save codes.** If you use Settings → Copy save code, the game creates a text code from your save so you can move it to another device. The code goes wherever you paste it. We don't see it.

## Playing online

Online play (the real Caravans, the Arena, the Wardens board and the Square) works only when the game can reach a backend: the claude.ai page a game link is shared from, or a Rainkeep server the app is set up with. Played offline, or from a copy with no backend, none of the following leaves your device.

When you play online, other players can see:

- **your keep's public profile:** your Rainwyrm's name, your keep's power, Rainwyrm level, skin, expedition stage, the heroes at home and their levels, your Arena points, your Caravan, and when you last played;
- **your name:** on a claude.ai link, the name on your claude.ai account as other people with access to the link see it (the game stores only an opaque ID, never the name itself); on a Rainkeep server, the name you choose in the game;
- **what you write in chat,** in your Caravan's chat or the Square, and your help requests, Arena battles and boss damage.

A Rainkeep server also keeps a device ID it creates for your account and, if you use it, a copy of your save so you can move it between devices. Chat can be reported to moderators.

## Playtests

When the game is played as part of a playtest from a shared link, it sends a gameplay report to the person running the test: the days you played, how long, which screens you opened, how far you got, your device type, screen size, graphics setting and frame rate, start-up times, any script errors, and the feedback notes you choose to send. The report is tied to the same opaque ID, is readable only by the test's owner, and contains no names, messages or anything you typed outside the Feedback box.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect, store or share:

- your email address, contacts or location
- advertising identifiers
- analytics for advertising or sale to anyone

The game has no ads.

## In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details.

To check and restore purchases, the app version of the game uses **RevenueCat**, a purchase-processing service. RevenueCat receives:

- an anonymous app user ID that it creates (not linked to your name or email)
- purchase receipts and transaction records from Apple or Google
- basic device information needed to process purchases (such as the app version, platform and country/currency for pricing)

RevenueCat uses this only to validate and restore purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

The web version of the game doesn't process real payments. Its store purchases are simulated.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a building finishes). These are created and delivered on your device. We don't run a notification server.

## Fonts and graphics

The game's typefaces and its 3D engine ship inside the game, so playing it doesn't contact any font or graphics service.

## Children

The game is rated 12+ and isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. Parents can turn off in-app purchases in their device's settings (Screen Time on iPhone, Family Link on Android).

## Deleting your data

Your save lives on your device. To delete it, use Settings → Start a new keep in the game, or delete the app. To have your online profile, messages and playtest report removed, contact us at the address below (on a claude.ai link, its owner can remove them).
