# Rainkeep privacy policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Rainkeep ("the game", "we") handles. In short: we don't collect your personal information. Your game is saved on your own device.

## What stays on your device

- **Your saved game.** Your keep, heroes, progress and settings are stored on your device (in the app's local storage). We never receive a copy.
- **Save codes.** If you use Settings → Copy save code, the game creates a text code from your save so you can move it to another device. The code goes wherever you paste it. We don't see it.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect, store or share:

- your name, email address, contacts or location
- advertising identifiers
- analytics or usage tracking
- crash reports sent to us

The game has no ads and no account or login.

## In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details.

To check and restore purchases, the app version of the game uses **RevenueCat**, a purchase-processing service. RevenueCat receives:

- an anonymous app user ID that it creates (not linked to your name or email)
- purchase receipts and transaction records from Apple or Google
- basic device information needed to process purchases (such as the app version, platform and country/currency for pricing)

RevenueCat uses this only to validate and restore purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

The web version of the game doesn't process real payments. Its store purchases are simulated.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a building finishes). These are created and delivered on your device. We don't run a notification server.

## Fonts and graphics

The game's typefaces and its 3D engine ship inside the game, so playing it doesn't contact any font or graphics service.

## Children

The game is rated 12+ and isn't directed at children under 13. Because we don't collect personal information, we don't knowingly collect information from children. Parents can turn off in-app purchases in their device's settings (Screen Time on iPhone, Family Link on Android).

## Deleting your data

Your data lives on your device. To delete it, use Settings → Start a new keep in the game, or delete the app.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
