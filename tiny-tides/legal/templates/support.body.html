Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game. This re-activates non-consumable purchases (Deep Ocean, Golden Hourglass, decor packs and the Starter Bundle) on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Sea Glass is consumable and can’t be restored. If a purchase went through but nothing arrived, leave the game open for a moment (it checks with the App Store on launch and when you return to it), then tap Restore purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase. We can’t see or refund App Store payments ourselves.

I lost my progress

Your game is saved on your device (and in your normal iOS backups), not on a server. If you deleted the app, got a new phone without restoring a backup, or used Reset progress, the save can’t be recovered — we’re sorry. Purchases can be restored, but pearls, creatures and collected toys can’t. Tip: keep iCloud Backup or a computer backup turned on.

The Sea Glass pulls are missing or greyed out

Sea Glass pulls can be switched off in Settings (turn on “Sea Glass capsule pulls” to use them), are limited to {{paidCap}} per day, and are not offered in some regions. Free daily capsules and Capsule Coins always work. All rates are in Capsule Machine → Rates and on the drop rates page.

Why didn’t my reminder arrive?

Reminders are optional. Turn them on in the game’s Settings and allow notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → Tiny Tides. We send at most three a day and never between 21:30 and 08:00. Focus modes and Low Power Mode can delay notifications.

My timers or rewards look wrong

Tiny Tides uses your device’s clock and time zone. Changing the date or time can shift timers, and the game protects itself from clock changes by not paying daily rewards twice. Set the clock to “Set Automatically” in iOS Settings.

Accessibility & comfort

In Settings you can turn off music, sound and haptics, turn on Reduce motion (also follows the iOS setting) and Battery saver. The game works in portrait and landscape on iPhone and iPad.

How do I delete my data?

The game keeps nothing on our servers. Delete the app (or use Settings → Reset progress) to erase everything it stored. See the Privacy Policy.
