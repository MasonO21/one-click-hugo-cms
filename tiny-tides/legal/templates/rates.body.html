How it works. Each capsule first lands in a tier (Common, Uncommon, Rare or Legendary) with the chance shown below, then one prize inside that tier is picked with the chance shown for that prize. Free capsules, Capsule Coin pulls and Sea Glass pulls all use exactly the same rates. Pulls are independent: earlier results don’t change later chances, except through the pity guarantee below.

Guarantee (pity)

  • At most {{pityRareMinus}} pulls in a row can pass without a Rare-or-better toy: your {{pityRare}}th pull is guaranteed to be a Rare or Legendary toy if you haven’t received one since your last. (Prize capsules — the small rewards shown below — never count as, or reset, the guarantee.)
  • At most {{pityLegendMinus}} pulls in a row can pass without a Legendary toy: your {{pityLegend}}th pull is guaranteed to be Legendary.
  • Getting a Rare or Legendary toy earlier restarts the relevant counter. The game shows your counters in the Capsule Machine. The guarantee only ever adds chances of better toys — it never lowers a rate shown here — so your real long-run chances of Rare and Legendary toys are slightly higher than the tier chances above.

Weekly spotlight

Every week one Rare toy and one Legendary toy are picked to be “in the spotlight”. A spotlighted toy is {{spotMult}} times as likely as it normally would be relative to the others in its tier. The tier chance itself does not change, so while one toy is boosted the other prizes in that tier are slightly less likely that week. The spotlight changes every week at 4 am local time and is shown in the Capsule Machine, whose Rates tab lists the exact chance of every prize for the current week.

Because the spotlight toy differs from week to week, a prize that is not in the spotlight has a small range of possible chances (from its lowest to its highest week); the tables below show that range, and the chance in the weeks the prize is itself the spotlight.
