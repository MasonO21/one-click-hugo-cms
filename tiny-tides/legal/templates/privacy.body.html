The short version. {{appName}} does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game is saved on your own device, and we never receive it.

Who we are

{{appName}} is published by {{developerName}} ({{address}}, {{country}}), referred to here as “we” and “us”. Contact: {{email}}.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number or address is requested by the app.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data is accessed.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) is read and no data is used for tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software is included in the app. The app makes no network requests of its own.

What is stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the app stores the following only on your device: your pool layout, creatures, toys and collection, currencies, settings (music, sound, haptics, reminders, reduce motion, battery saver, Sea Glass pulls on/off), tutorial progress, timestamps used to work out progress while you were away, and identifiers of purchases already delivered (so a purchase is never credited twice). This data is held in the app’s private storage and may be included in your normal iOS device backups (for example iCloud Backup) according to your own device settings. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it; you can also use Settings → Reset progress in the app.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your Apple ID, payment card or billing details. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit framework on your device to confirm what you have bought (for example, to restore your purchases). Apple’s handling of your data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy. Purchase records that Apple provides to developers (such as sales and units) are aggregated and do not identify you to us.

Reminders (notifications)

If you switch reminders on, the app asks iOS for permission and then schedules local notifications on your device (for example when a Tide Gift arrives or an evolution finishes). They are created and delivered by your device. We do not operate a push-notification server and do not receive a push token. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Sharing pictures

When you tap Share, the app creates a picture of your tidepool or a creature on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You decide whether and where to send it. We do not receive it.

Capsule Machine

Capsule prizes are chosen on your device using the published drop rates. The results of your pulls stay on your device.

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted by {{hostingProvider}}. We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or social-media trackers, and we load no third-party scripts or fonts. Like most web hosts, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) for security and operations; we do not access or use these logs to identify visitors.

If you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and to improve the game, keep it for up to 24 months (longer only if the law requires), and do not share it except with our email provider as needed to send and receive messages. Please do not send sensitive information.

Children

{{appName}} is a general-audience game. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (US COPPA), under 16 (EU GDPR-K, where applicable) or from any other age group, and there is no chat, social feature or user-generated content. Purchases require an Apple ID; parents can use Apple’s Ask to Buy, Screen Time and Family Sharing controls, and can switch off Sea Glass capsule pulls in the app’s Settings. See our Parents’ guide.
