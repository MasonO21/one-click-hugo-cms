Parents’ guide

Tiny Tides is a calm collecting game about a tidepool. This page explains what’s in it and how to keep spending under your control.

What’s in the game

  • No ads. No third-party advertising or analytics.
  • No accounts, chat or social features. Players can’t contact each other or share anything except a picture they choose to send through the iOS share sheet.
  • No personal data collected. Nothing leaves the device (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Optional purchases for cosmetic decor, time-savers, the Deep Ocean area and Sea Glass. The game is fully playable without paying.
  • Random capsules. The Capsule Machine is a “gashapon” toy machine. It gives a free capsule every day and uses free Capsule Coins earned by playing. Capsule pulls can also be bought with Sea Glass; the prizes are chosen at random.

About the random capsules

  • Every prize and its exact chance are published in the game and on our drop rates page before any purchase.
  • Prizes are cosmetic toys and small one-time rewards; they can’t be sold or exchanged for money.
  • A Rare-or-better toy is guaranteed at least every 10 pulls and a Legendary at least every 60 pulls.
  • A pull costs 30 Sea Glass. The game allows at most 20 Sea Glass pulls per day, asks for confirmation before each purchase, and never sells anything during the tutorial.
  • You can turn Sea Glass pulls off completely: open Settings in the game and switch off “Sea Glass capsule pulls”. Free capsules and Capsule Coins keep working.
  • Some regions don’t get Sea Glass pulls at all.

Controlling spending on iPhone and iPad

  1. Block in-app purchases. On your child’s device: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases → In-app Purchases → Don’t Allow. (Turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions first.)
  2. Require approval for every purchase. With Family Sharing, turn on Ask to Buy for your child: Settings → [your name] → Family Sharing → [child] → Ask To Buy. You’ll get a request each time.
  3. Require a password. Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → Password Settings → Always Require.
  4. Set limits. Use Screen Time’s App Limits and Downtime for time in the app.
  5. Switch off Sea Glass pulls inside the game (see above).

Apple’s own guide: support.apple.com/HT201304 (manage in-app purchases and spending).

If a purchase was made by mistake

Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Refunds are decided by Apple; we can’t see or reverse App Store payments.

Age rating

The App Store age rating reflects the optional random purchases. Tiny Tides contains no violence, scary content, gambling with real money, or user-generated content. It is a general-audience game and is not designed specifically for children under 13. Parents of younger players should use the controls above.
