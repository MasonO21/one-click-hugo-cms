Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings (the gear icon) → Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy.

My creatures didn’t earn pearls while I was away.

They do, up to the size of their bubbles (4 hours at first, growing with your Pool Level). Pop your bubbles every few hours so they never stop growing.

How do I get a new form?

Level a creature up, then arrange rocks, plants and water near it. The strongest nearby trait decides what it becomes. Tap the creature and open the Evolution tab to see what each form needs.

Can I move to a new phone?

Your save lives on your device and in your iOS device backups. Restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup to bring your tidepool with you.

What are my chances in the Capsule Machine?

Open the Capsule Machine and tap Rates. It lists every prize with its exact percentage, the guarantees, and the weekly Spotlight. You can also switch Sea Glass pulls off in Settings. A free capsule is available every day.

How do reminders work?

Turn them on in Settings. They are local notifications: at most a few a day, never overnight.
