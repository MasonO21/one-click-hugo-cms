Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The short version: Tiny Tides does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

Your tidepool, creatures, settings and progress are saved locally on your device (and included in your normal iOS device backups, if you use them). We never receive this data. Deleting the app deletes the saved game.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (Sea Glass, cosmetic decor bundles, the Golden Hourglass and the Deep Ocean biome) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment information or Apple ID. The app asks the App Store which purchases you own so that "Restore Purchases" works.

Notifications

If you choose to turn on reminders, Tiny Tides schedules local notifications on your device (for example, when a Tide Gift arrives or an evolution finishes). They are created on your device and nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in the app Settings or in iOS Settings.

Sharing pictures

If you tap Share, the app creates a picture of your tidepool on your device and gives it to the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes. We never upload it.

Children

Tiny Tides is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no links out of the app except to this website and Apple’s own pages.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. It uses Apple frameworks (StoreKit, UserNotifications) and open-source libraries that run entirely on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add optional online features), we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before releasing the update. Questions: see the Support page.
