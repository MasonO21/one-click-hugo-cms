## Description (4,000 max) · 2307 characters

```
The sun went grey and never warmed again. In the ash of a fallen forge, you found the last living flame: a hatchling fire dragon. Its warmth is all that stands between your people and the frost.

Raise your Hearthwyrm from a hungry Hatchling to a Primordial wyrm that blazes like a small sun. Feed it coal, choose how hot it burns, and watch it grow through seven forms. At its Ascension, pick the fire it will carry for good.

SURVIVAL THAT STAYS REAL
• Read the forecast and prepare. Blizzards and Deep Freezes arrive on a schedule you can see coming.
• Bank the fire on calm days to save coal, then let it roar when the storm hits.
• Keep your survivors fed, sheltered and warm. Cold makes people sick; an Infirmary brings them back.
• The frost hunts warmth. The bigger your wyrm grows, the harder the storms come for it.

BUILD YOUR HOLD
• Twelve buildings around the nest, from Coal Pits and Hunter's Lodges to the Archive of Thaw and the Kindred Hall.
• Assign workers, research ten technologies and guard your stores against raiders.

HEROES WITH A JOB AT HOME
• Recruit 18 heroes across three classes: Shieldguards, Frostbows and Sled Lancers.
• Station heroes as Stewards to boost a building, then lead them into battle.
• Recruitment odds are always shown, with a guaranteed Legendary within 40 recruits.

EXPLORE THE SNOWFIELD
• Send marches to gather timber, coal, food and iron.
• Hunt beasts, burn raider camps, and explore ruins where your choices decide what your scouts bring home.
• Push through six chapters of story, from Whitepine Edge to the Heart of Winter, then keep pushing the endless Frostline.

STAND WITH YOUR KINDRED
• Join a Kindred whose members speed up your upgrades, fund shared research and send gifts.
• Strike the Titan in raids that open every few hours.
• Climb the Thaw Wars bracket, matched so you face rivals who play like you.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Your hold keeps working while you're away, and your wyrm banks its fire so no one falls ill.
• Daily duties, a gift calendar, achievements, events and the Hearthkeeper's Ledger season pass.
• Cosmetic dragon skins change how your wyrm looks and nothing else.

Kindlehold is free to play with optional in-app purchases, including items that give random heroes. You can turn off in-app purchases in your device settings.
```
