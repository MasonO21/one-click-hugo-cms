*Last updated: September 29, 2026*

This policy covers the Trail Notes website. It does not cover the Trail Notes app, which is not released yet. We will publish a separate policy for the app before it launches.

## What we collect

- **Waitlist.** If you join the waitlist, we collect your email address.
- **Contact form.** If you send us a message, we collect your name (optional), your email address, and your message.
- **Hosting logs.** Our host, Netlify, processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.

## How we use it

We use your email address to tell you when Trail Notes is ready. We use contact messages to reply to you. We do not sell your information.

## Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Fonts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact a font provider. The home page loads a script from Netlify that is used to sign in to the site’s admin area. Visitors do not need to use it.

## Service providers

Form submissions are received and stored by Netlify Forms, and the site is hosted by Netlify. Their handling of data is described in the [Netlify privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information, or to stop emailing you, at any time using the [contact form](/contact/).
