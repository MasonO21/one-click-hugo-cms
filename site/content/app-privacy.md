*Last updated: September 29, 2026*

This policy covers the Trail Notes app for iPhone and Android. For the website, see the [website privacy policy](/privacy/).

## The short version

Your notes stay on your phone. We do not run a server for the app, we have no account system, and we never receive your notes, recordings, or location. The app has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

## What the app stores on your phone

- **Notes.** The text of what you say or write, and the time you made the note.
- **Tags.** If you allow location, each note can also store the coordinates where you were, a place name, the weather at that time, and a mood the app estimates from your words. You can change the mood at any time.
- **Outings.** If you start an outing, the app stores its name, start and end times, distance, and the route points it recorded. It records your route only while the app is open.
- **Settings.** Your unit and recording preferences.

The app does not save audio. Your voice is turned into text as you speak, and only the text is kept.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include the app's data if you have backups turned on.

## What the app sends off your phone

- **Speech recognition.** Your phone's speech recognition service turns your voice into text. By default the app asks your phone to do this on the device. If your phone or language does not support that, or if you choose to allow online recognition in Settings, your audio may be processed by Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) under their privacy policies. We do not receive it.
- **Weather.** To add the weather, the app sends a location rounded to one decimal place (about 11 km) to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms). Open-Meteo also receives your device's IP address. It does not receive your notes.
- **Place names.** Your phone's built-in map service (Apple or Google) turns your coordinates into a place name, under its own privacy policy.

If you deny location access, the app still works. Notes then have no place, weather, or route.

## Permissions

- **Microphone and speech recognition:** to hear the notes you record.
- **Location while using the app:** to save where you were, add the weather, and record an outing's route. The app never asks for background location.

You can change these at any time in your phone's Settings.

## Your choices

- **Export.** Settings has an option to export your notes as text or JSON.
- **Delete.** Settings has an option to delete every note and outing from the app. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.
- **Tags.** You can edit or clear the mood on any note, and delete any note or outing.

## Children

Trail Notes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this page and its date.
