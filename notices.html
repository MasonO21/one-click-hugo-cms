1. Fonts

Three fonts are bundled inside the game as files, so the game does not download them. They come from Google Fonts by way of Fontsource, and are included as Latin-subset WOFF2 files. Each one is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The copyright notice for each font, as given in the license file that comes with it, and the full license text follow. The license applies to the fonts. It does not apply to the rest of the game.

2. Artwork and sounds

All of the game's artwork and sounds are original. The artwork is vector and canvas artwork drawn in code, and the sound effects and music are original compositions synthesized on your device while you play. The game contains no third-party art, music or sound files.

3. Apple system frameworks

The game uses only frameworks that Apple provides as part of iOS: UIKit, WebKit, StoreKit, SafariServices and AVFoundation. They are not distributed inside the game, and they are used under the terms that apply to iOS and to Apple's developer program. The game contains no other third-party code.

4. Trademarks

Apple, iPhone, iPad and App Store are trademarks of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries and regions. Other product and company names mentioned on these pages may be trademarks of their respective owners. Night Precinct is not sponsored or endorsed by Apple.

5. Fiction and non-affiliation

Night Precinct is a work of fiction. The characters, places, organizations and events in it, including its three worlds (Night Precinct, Ember Station and Golden Hour), are invented and shown in a cartoon style for entertainment. The game is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any real police, fire or EMS agency, department or organization, and any resemblance to a real person or organization is a coincidence. The game is not medical, legal or safety advice. See the Terms of Use.
