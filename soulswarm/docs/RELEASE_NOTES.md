# SOULSWARM release notes

Store-ready "What's New" copy (App Store: up to 4,000 characters; Google Play: up to 500 per language), newest first. The launch build ships everything below as version 1.0, so its listing uses the **1.0** text; the per-update notes are kept for the live updates that re-introduce content after soft launch (`LIVEOPS.md`).

## 1.0: launch

**Google Play (≤ 500 characters):**

> Raise the legion! Every foe you slay can rise to fight for you. 30 chapters in six cursed realms, ten painted bosses, eight Shepherds with their own Rites, Nightmare and Torment, the Endless Abyss, the weekly Boss Rush, a 30-tier Soul Pass and 63 Feats to earn. Portrait, one thumb, 6–9 minute runs.

(Check the length against the console after edits: Google counts characters, not bytes.)

**App Store:**

> **Raise the legion.** Every foe you slay can rise again as a soul that fights for you. Grow from 1 to 300+, pick the right Soul Gate and wipe the screen with Soul Nova.
>
> • **30 chapters, six realms:** drowned coasts, a thornwood, plague fens, a storm spire and the first night itself, each with its own hazard and foe.
> • **Ten bosses**, each with three phases. Old foes return stronger.
> • **Eight Shepherds**, each with a signature weapon, a passive and a Rite. Master them to ascend their Rites.
> • **Build every run:** 9 weapons, 10 passives, 9 evolutions and Soul Unions; reroll or banish what you don't want.
> • **Endgame:** Nightmare and Torment on every chapter, the Endless Abyss, the weekly Boss Rush (the Hollow and Fallen Courts), Blood Moon weekends and the Daily Trial.
> • **Goals:** a 30-tier Soul Pass, 63 Feats, a painted Bestiary, Hero Mastery and Relic Ascension.
> • **Fair by design:** odds shown before every summon, rewarded ads always optional, every chapter beatable without paying.
