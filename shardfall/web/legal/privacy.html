Ads and tracking

Ads appear only when you choose to watch a video for a reward. On iOS we ask your permission before using the advertising identifier, and you can change your answer in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. If you decline, you still get rewards, but the ads are less personalised.

Purchases and spending controls

The game shows chest drop rates before purchase. You can set a monthly spending limit in Settings, and purchases that would go over it are blocked. Parents can also use Screen Time (iOS) or Family Link (Android) to require approval for purchases.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep online account data while your account is active. To delete everything, open Settings → Delete my data in the game. This erases your progress on the device and deletes your account and cloud save from our server. You can also email us. Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you can ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, or object to how it is used. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Online play and our API use encrypted connections (TLS). No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect and who can access it.
