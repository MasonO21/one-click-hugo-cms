## Description

Trail Notes is a voice journal for hikers and runners.

Tap Record and talk while you move. Trail Notes writes down what you said and adds the place, the weather, and your mood, so you can find any day again.

TALK, DON'T TYPE
Record a note in one tap. No typing with cold hands.

AUTOMATIC TAGS
Every note is tagged with where you were, what the weather was doing, and a mood picked up from your words. You can change the mood any time.

TRACK AN OUTING
Start a hike or run to save your route and distance. Notes you make along the way are kept with it.

SEARCH IT ALL
Look up "foggy ridge" or "felt strong" and find the right day.

YOURS ALONE
Your notes stay on your phone. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking. Export a copy or delete everything whenever you like.

Keep Trail Notes open while you are out. Route tracking works while the app is open.

## Suggested privacy answers

Check these against the final build before submitting.

### Apple: App Privacy

- Data used to track you: none.
- Data collected: **Coarse Location**, not linked to you, not used for tracking, used for App Functionality. A location rounded to about 11 km is sent to the weather service. This matches `ios.privacyManifests` in `app.config.ts`.
- Notes, outings, and precise location stay on the device and are not collected by the developer.
- Speech recognition is performed by Apple's own service and is covered by Apple's terms.

### Google Play: Data safety

- Data shared with third parties: **Approximate location**, to Open-Meteo, for app functionality. Rounded to about 11 km.
- Data collected by the developer: none. Everything else stays on the device.
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Users can delete their data from within the app.
- Speech recognition on Android may be processed by Google's service when on-device recognition is unavailable.
- The app has no ads and no analytics.
