Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for review. This text describes how Sunup works today. Have a lawyer review it, and fill in the bracketed company details, before launch.

The short version

  • Sunup collects what it needs to know whether you're okay and to reach the people you chose.
  • Sunup doesn't track your location. Your location is only sent when you press SOS with "Include my location" on.
  • We don't sell your information or show ads.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, mobile number, time zone, and a sign-in token for each device.
  • Schedule: your check-in windows, grace period, and away-mode dates.
  • Check-ins: when you checked in, and any mood, note or photo you add.
  • Your circle: who watches over you and whom you watch. For people you add by phone, their name, number and whether they replied YES or STOP.
  • "If I go dark" packet: whatever you write in it (for example pet care, door codes, medications and contacts) and who may receive it.
  • Moments and SOS: timer details you enter (who, where, a link, notes), and your location only if you share it with an SOS.
  • Messages: a log of the notifications, texts and calls Sunup sends about you, which you can see under Activity.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and status, never your card number.
  • Notifications: your browser's push subscription, so we can alert you.

How we use it

To run your check-ins, send reminders and alerts, show your circle your check-ins, release your packet when an alert reaches that step, sign you in, process subscriptions, and keep the service secure. We don't use your information for advertising.

Who can see what

  • People watching over you see your check-ins (with mood, note and photo), your number, and alerts about you.
  • People you add by phone only receive the texts and calls Sunup sends about you, including your packet if you marked them to receive it.
  • Your packet is only visible to the people you mark, only after an alert reaches the release step, and it seals again when the alert ends.

Service providers

We use Twilio to send texts and make calls, Stripe for payments, your browser's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) for notifications, and [hosting provider] to run Sunup. They process data only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

  • Check-ins, resolved alerts and ended timers: 60 days.
  • The log of messages sent: 30 days.
  • Account, circle and packet: until you delete them or your account.

Your choices

  • Edit or clear your packet, remove people from your circle, or pause check-ins at any time in the app.
  • People added by phone can reply STOP to stop all texts and calls.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or for your account to be deleted by emailing [support email].

Security

Connections are encrypted, sign-in tokens are stored only as one-way hashes, and sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at [support email] if you find a problem.

Children

Sunup isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
