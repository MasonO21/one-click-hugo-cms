What Sunup is

Sunup ("we") is a daily check-in service. You choose a time window each day to tell Sunup you're okay. If you don't, Sunup reminds you and then contacts the people you chose (your "circle") by app notification, text message and phone call. These terms are an agreement between you and [Company name].

Sunup is not an emergency service

Sunup does not dispatch police, fire or medical help, and it does not monitor you continuously. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Sunup depends on things we don't control: your phone's battery, signal, settings and notification permissions; mobile carriers; and the services we use to send texts, calls and notifications. Messages can be delayed or fail. We can't guarantee that any reminder, alert, text or call will be delivered, or that anyone in your circle will respond.

Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Sunup.
  • Keep your phone number current. If you sign in with your number, anyone who controls that number can sign in to your account.
  • Keep notifications turned on, and keep your check-in window and away-mode settings accurate. Sunup acts on the schedule you set.

Your circle

  • Only add people who know you and agree to be contacted about you. When you add someone by phone, Sunup texts them to confirm, and they can reply STOP at any time to stop all texts and calls from Sunup.
  • People who watch over you can see your check-ins, mood notes and photos. Information in your "If I go dark" packet is only shown to the people you mark, and only while an alert has reached the release step.
  • Don't use Sunup to harass, track or monitor anyone without their knowledge.
