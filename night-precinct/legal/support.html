Reporting a bug

The more we know, the faster we can find the problem. Please include:

  • Your device model, for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air (5th generation).
  • Your iOS version. You can find it in the iOS Settings app under General, then About.
  • The app version and build. They are shown at the bottom of Settings in the game.
  • The steps that led to the problem, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. A screenshot can help.
  • For a purchase problem: the product, the date and the Order ID from the receipt email Apple sent you.

Please do not send passwords or payment card numbers. We will never need them.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the game, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. You need an internet connection, and you must be signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchases. This restores one-time purchases (the Rookie Starter Pack, Career Pass Premium, the Auto-Clicker, the Combo Auto-Clicker and the upgrade) and an active Chief's Club subscription. Apple does not keep consumable purchases (Gold Badge packs, the Evidence Safe and the Daily Deals) as restorable, so Restore Purchases cannot bring those back. See the Purchase Terms.

I bought something but it did not arrive, or my Gold Badges are missing

  1. Check that Apple charged you. Look for Apple's receipt email or your purchase history in your Apple Account. If there is no charge, the purchase did not go through. If the purchase is shown as pending, for example because it is waiting for Ask to Buy approval, the game delivers it once it is approved.
  2. Close the game completely and open it again while you are online. Each time it starts, the game checks with the App Store for purchases that have not been delivered yet, and delivers them.
  3. If it is a one-time purchase or Chief's Club, tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
  4. If it is still missing, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the details listed under "Reporting a bug", including the Order ID. We cannot see your payment records, so we will need those details. If we cannot fix it, Apple can refund the purchase (see below).

Please do not delete the game until the problem is sorted out, because that could remove your progress.

How do I cancel Chief's Club?

You cancel with Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, choose Chief's Club and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the game's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, or you will be charged for the next one. You keep the benefits until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription. See the Purchase Terms for the full details.

How do refunds work?

Apple takes your payment, so Apple handles refunds, and we cannot issue them. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, choose the purchase and follow the steps. Apple decides each request. See the Purchase Terms.

What happens to my progress on a new phone, or if I reinstall the game?

The game has no account and no cloud sync. Your progress is saved only on your device, and it is included in normal iOS device backups, whether to iCloud or to a computer. If you set up a new device by restoring from a backup that includes the game, your progress comes with it. If you delete the game and install it again without a backup, or your backup does not include the game, your progress is gone and you start again. Before you change phones, check that you have a recent backup. After restoring, tap Restore Purchases in Settings to unlock your one-time purchases and Chief's Club. Gold Badges you bought are part of your saved game, so they come back only with your backup. We cannot recover a lost saved game.

How do I erase all my progress?

Go to Settings in the game and tap Erase Save. This erases your saved progress from the game and cannot be undone. It does not cancel a Chief's Club subscription, and it does not change what Apple has recorded as your purchases, so you can restore one-time purchases and an active subscription afterwards. A backup made before you erased may still contain your old progress until a newer backup replaces it or you delete it.

How do offline earnings work?

While the game is closed, it keeps earning for you for a limited time, up to a cap. When you open the game again, it works out how long you have been away from the time saved in your game and your device's clock, and pays out what you earned, up to the cap. You can raise the cap with Gold Badges (upgrades of 4, 8 or 24 hours), and you can double or triple a payout for 6 or 15 Gold Badges. Chief's Club pays offline earnings at 100% with a cap of at least 8 hours. Changing your device's date or time can change what you are paid.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game makes no network requests of its own and plays fully offline. You only need a connection to make or restore a purchase, to manage your subscription, or to open one of the web pages linked from the game.

Why does the game never ask me to log in or show ads?

Your progress lives on your device, so the game does not need to know who you are and has no account or sign-in. It also shows no ads and uses no ad networks. The game is supported by optional purchases. In place of ads, it has free rewards you can claim whenever you like, such as Rally Boost, the Supply Drop and the Daily Roll Call. See the Privacy Policy for what the game does and does not do with your information.

Why can't I buy crates with Gold Badges?

The game turns off buying crates with Gold Badges when your device's region is set to Belgium. This affects only buying crates with Gold Badges. The odds for all crates are on the Crate Odds and Rewards page.

Accessibility

We want as many people as possible to be able to enjoy {{APP_NAME}}. These options are in the game's Settings:

  • Reduce Flashing to cut down on flashing effects.
  • Sound can be turned off. The game's sounds are short beeps made on your device.
  • Haptics (vibration feedback) can be turned off.

The game keeps earning while it is closed (see offline earnings above), and the Auto-Clicker, an optional purchase, taps for you.

Accessibility statement. We have not claimed that the game meets any particular accessibility standard, and some parts of it may be difficult to use with assistive technology such as VoiceOver, Voice Control or Switch Control. If you run into a barrier, please tell us what you were trying to do and what device and assistive technology you use. We take this feedback seriously, and we will try to improve the game where we can. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We aim to reply within a few business days.
