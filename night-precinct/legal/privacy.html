2. What this policy covers

3. What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can carry on where you left off. This saved game is the only data the game creates, and it never leaves your device unless your own device backup includes it (see section 8).

The saved game is kept in two places inside the game's private storage on your device: in the local storage of the game's built-in web view, and mirrored to a small file called save.json in the game's Application Support folder. There is no cloud sync.

What the saved game contains
What is storedWhy
Game state: your currencies (cash and Gold Badges), upgrades and other progress So the game can run and you keep your progress between sessions.
Timestamps, such as when you last played and when timers and boosts started To work out offline earnings and to run the timers for free rewards, boosts and jobs.
Settings, such as sound effects, music, haptics and Reduce Flashing To remember the choices you made in Settings.
A list of recent purchase transaction IDs (Apple's reference numbers for your recent purchases), and how many Gold Badges each of those purchases gave So that a purchase is delivered once and never twice, and so that the Gold Badges from a purchase that Apple refunds can be taken back.
A running total of purchases, counted in US-dollar reference values To work out your permanent Precinct Patron income bonus (see Purchase Terms). It is calculated on your device from a fixed list of reference values, not from anything Apple reports to us.

The saved game does not contain your name, email address, Apple Account details, payment details or location. We do not have access to it.

While it is running, the game also reads a few device settings to work properly. It reads your device's region and language, and asks Apple's StoreKit for the country of your App Store account (only the country code), to apply the country rules for crates described in Purchase Terms. The game is currently available in English only; it reads the language so that it can follow your device language if a translation is added. It also reads whether Reduce Motion is switched on in iOS so it can adapt to it. These settings are used on the device and are not sent anywhere.

The game does not ask for any device permissions.

4. What we receive

4.1 From the game: nothing

The game makes no network requests of its own. Its fonts are bundled inside the app, so it downloads nothing. It sends nothing to us and nothing to any third party. This matches the "Data Not Collected" label in the App Privacy section of the game's App Store page.

The only network activity connected to the game is:

  • Apple's own App Store traffic between your device and Apple when you make or restore a purchase or manage a subscription (see section 5); and
  • opening a web page in a browser view when you tap a link (see section 6).

4.2 When you contact us

If you email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} or {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}, we receive your email address, your name if it appears in your email, what you write and any attachments or technical details you choose to include (for example your device model and iOS version). We use this only to reply to you, to solve the problem you raised, and to keep a reasonable record of the conversation. We do not use it for marketing and we do not add you to any mailing list.

  • Legal basis (EEA and UK): our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us and running the game properly, or taking steps at your request that relate to your purchases or use of the game. If we must keep something to meet a legal obligation, that is the basis for keeping it.
  • Who sees it: we, and the email provider that hosts our mailbox, acting on our behalf. We do not disclose it to anyone else unless the law requires us to, or it is needed to establish or defend a legal claim.
  • How long we keep it: see section 8.

4.3 From Apple

Apple gives every app publisher reports about their apps. These include sales and download figures by country or region and, for people who have chosen to share diagnostics and usage data with app developers in their device settings, anonymous crash and usage reports. We receive these reports from Apple, not from the game. They are aggregated or anonymous. They do not include your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details. We use them to see how the game is doing, to fix crashes, and for tax and accounting.

4.4 These web pages

These pages are plain static pages. They contain no scripts, load nothing from other websites and do not track you. Like any website, the company that hosts these pages may keep ordinary technical server logs (such as IP address, date and time, and the page requested) so that it can keep the site secure and running. We do not use those logs to identify or profile players.

5. Purchases and Apple

All purchases in the game are Apple In-App Purchases made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, collects any taxes, keeps your purchase history, handles refunds and, if you use Family Sharing, handles Ask to Buy approvals. We never see your card number, billing address or other payment details, and we do not see your Apple Account.

The game receives from Apple's StoreKit only the prices of its products, the product ID, transaction ID and date of your purchases, and the country code of your App Store account (see section 3), so that it can show prices, deliver what you bought, avoid delivering it twice and apply the country rules for crates. These stay on your device (see section 3).

How Apple handles your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Apple's terms, not by this policy.

6. Links the game can open

When you tap a link in the game, the game opens a web page for you in a browser view provided by iOS. The pages it can open are our own legal and support pages (the pages listed on the home page) and Apple's subscription management page. The game has no general web browser.

Once a page opens, it is a website like any other. The site can see ordinary things such as your IP address and browser type, and it is governed by its own privacy policy. For Apple's page, that is Apple's policy. For our pages, see section 4.4. The game does not add any information about you or your saved game to these links, and we cannot see what you do in the browser view.

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing to collect from a child through the game. What a parent or guardian should know

  • The game has optional in-app purchases, including randomized crates. Real money can be spent on Gold Badges and on other items. All of it is optional, and crates can also be earned for free. See the Purchase Terms and the crate odds.
  • Ask to Buy: if you use Apple Family Sharing, you can turn on Ask to Buy for a child's account, so that every purchase request is sent to you for approval before it goes through.
  • Screen Time: on your child's device you can go to Settings, then Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions, and set In-App Purchases to Don't Allow. You can also require a password for every purchase. Steps are in the Purchase Terms.
  • The game has no ads, and its only links go to our own pages and to Apple's subscription page.
  • You can erase your child's progress in the game at any time with Erase Save in Settings, or by deleting the game.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: your saved game stays for as long as the game is installed. Choose Settings, then Erase Save in the game to erase your saved progress. Deleting the game from your device deletes its data from the device.
  • Backups: your saved game is included in normal iOS device backups, whether to iCloud or to a computer. Whether a backup exists, and how long it is kept, is controlled by your own Apple, iCloud and computer backup settings. We cannot see, change or delete your backups. A backup made before you erased or deleted the game may still contain your old progress until a newer backup replaces it or you delete it.
  • On our side: we do not hold a copy of your saved game, so there is nothing for us to delete or return.
  • Emails to us: we keep them only as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up, and then delete them. If we have to keep something longer, for example to meet a legal obligation or to establish or defend a legal claim, we keep only what is needed for that purpose.

9. Security

Because we run no servers that store player data, there is no database of players that could be breached. Your saved game is protected by the private storage that iOS gives the game and by the passcode and encryption you have set up on your device. The game does not add its own encryption to the saved game, so keep your device and your backups secure. Payment security is Apple's responsibility. Email is not a fully secure way to send information, so please do not send us anything sensitive. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

Where the law requires safeguards for transfers of personal data out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Your rights and choices

Because the game sends us no personal information, we hold no personal information about you from the game. This means we cannot look up your saved game, and there is nothing from the game for us to correct, export or delete. The rights below still apply to you, and we will answer any request. The only personal information we might hold is what you have emailed us.

11.1 Control over your device data

Everything the game stores is on your device, and you control it. You can erase it with Erase Save in Settings or by deleting the game, and you control any backups through your Apple and iCloud settings (see section 8).

11.2 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR and UK GDPR)

You have the right to ask us for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to our use of it, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw any consent you gave. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). Please contact us first so that we can try to put things right.

11.3 California (CCPA and CPRA)

If you live in California you have the right to know what personal information we have collected about you, to ask us to delete or correct it, to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be treated unfairly for using these rights.

  • Categories collected in the last 12 months: from the game, none. From emails sent to us, identifiers (your email address and name if you give it) and the content of your message.
  • Sold or shared: we do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the last 12 months, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not collect it.
  • We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing.

11.4 Other US states

Residents of other US states with privacy laws (for example Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas, Utah and Virginia) have rights that are similar in kind: to know, access, correct and delete personal data, and to opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising and certain profiling. We do none of those things. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the word "Appeal", and we will respond within the time your state's law requires. You can also contact your state attorney general.

11.5 Brazil (LGPD) and other countries

Under Brazil's LGPD and similar laws elsewhere you may ask us to confirm whether we process your personal data, to give you access to it, to correct it, to anonymize, block or delete it, to make it portable, to tell you who it has been shared with, and to review any consent you gave. In Brazil you may also complain to the national data protection authority (ANPD).

11.6 How to make a request

Email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and say which right you want to use and where you live. Please write from the email address you used when you contacted us. To protect your privacy we may ask you to confirm that you control that address before we act. An authorized agent may make a request for you if they can show they have your permission. There is no charge. We will answer within the time the law requires where you live (generally one month for GDPR and UK GDPR requests, and 45 days for California requests, with extensions where the law allows). If we hold nothing about you, we will tell you so.

12. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions about you by automated means, and we do not profile you. The game applies fixed rules on your device, such as switching off paid random rewards when your App Store country or device region is Belgium or Brazil, and it uses random draws for crates. These are rules and chance, they are not decisions about you personally, and nothing about them is sent to us.
