3. Virtual currency and items

"Gold Badges" are the game's premium currency. Gold Badges, crates, units, upgrades, gear, cosmetics, boosts and every other item or benefit in the game are "virtual items". You should know that:

  • You get a license, not ownership. When you buy or earn a virtual item, you get a limited, personal, non-transferable, revocable license to use it inside the game under these terms and the Terms of Use. You do not own it.
  • No cash value. Virtual items have no cash value and are not money, property or a stored-value balance.
  • They cannot be transferred, sold, or exchanged. You cannot give, sell, trade or transfer virtual items to another person, to another device or for money or anything of value, and they cannot be redeemed for cash or for anything outside the game.
  • They can be lost. Your virtual items are kept only in your saved game on your device. We cannot see or restore them. If you delete the game, or lose or reset your device, without a backup, they are lost. If you use Erase Save, they are erased on purpose. See the Support page for how backups work.
  • Corrections. We may correct a balance only to fix a genuine error (for example a bug that gave you or took away the wrong amount), and any correction would be made through an update to the game.

What Gold Badges can be used for. Inside the game you can spend Gold Badges on crates, instant job completion, time warps, double-time boosts, offline-earnings cap upgrades (4, 8 or 24 hours), extra job slots, elite recruits (permanent multipliers), cosmetics (vehicle skins and skylines), restoring a broken daily streak, and doubling or tripling your offline earnings (for 6 or 15 Gold Badges).

Free ways to earn Gold Badges. You can also earn Gold Badges for free through achievements, the daily roll call, timed jobs, bounty targets, the free track of the Career Pass, crates, and a small amount from leveling up. The game also has free rewards you can claim: Rally Boost (a free 2x income boost for 15 minutes, available every 3 hours), Supply Drop (a free standard crate every 6 hours) and the Daily Roll Call (a 7-day streak calendar; a broken streak can be restored for 15 Gold Badges or restarted for free).

4. Delivery and restoring purchases

  • Delivery. After you confirm a purchase with Apple, the game delivers it automatically. If delivery is interrupted (for example, the game is closed or your connection drops), the game checks with the App Store for undelivered purchases each time it starts, and delivers them then. If Apple shows a purchase as pending, for example while it waits for approval through Ask to Buy, it is delivered once it is approved.
  • Consumables cannot be restored. Gold Badge packs, the Evidence Safe and the Daily Deals are consumables. Apple does not keep them as restorable purchases, and Restore Purchases will not bring them back. Once delivered, they exist only in your saved game (see section 3).
  • Non-consumables and the subscription can be restored. Open the game, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. The game asks the App Store which non-consumable purchases and which active Chief's Club subscription belong to your Apple Account, and unlocks them again on your device. You may be asked to sign in to your Apple Account. One-time contents of a purchase, such as the Gold Badges and crates in the Rookie Starter Pack, are delivered when you buy it and are part of your saved game.

5. Chief's Club subscription

Chief's Club is an auto-renewable subscription. The details are:

  • Name: Chief's Club (subscription product vip_weekly).
  • Length: one week. It renews every week until you cancel it.
  • Price: the weekly price shown in the game on the purchase screen, in your currency. Apple shows the price again before you confirm.
  • What you get while it is active: all income x1.5; offline earnings at 100% with an offline earnings cap of at least 8 hours; one extra job slot; bounties collect automatically; and 25 Gold Badges per day. Gold Badges you have already received stay in your balance if the subscription ends.

Subscription terms

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period, at the price of the plan.
  • You can manage and cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings on the App Store after purchase.
  • Deleting the game does not cancel your subscription. You must cancel it with Apple.

How to cancel

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions. Or, in the game, go to Settings and tap Manage Subscription.
  2. Choose Chief's Club.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm. Do this at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, or you will be charged for the next one.

If you cancel, you keep the Chief's Club benefits until the end of the period you have already paid for. When you cancel part-way through a period, we do not refund the unused part of that period, except where the law requires it or where Apple approves a refund under its own process (see section 6).

Price changes

We may change the weekly price of Chief's Club. If we do, Apple tells you in the way Apple's rules require (for example by email or notification) before the new price applies to you. Where Apple or the law requires your consent to a price increase, your subscription will not continue at the new price unless you agree. You can cancel before the change takes effect. A price change never affects a period you have already paid for.

6. Refunds and your right to withdraw

Refunds are handled by Apple

Apple takes your payment, so Apple handles refunds, not us. We do not have your payment details or your purchase records, and we cannot issue a refund ourselves. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, choose the purchase and follow the steps. Apple decides refund requests under its own terms. If a purchase did not arrive, see the Support page first, and contact us if you need help.

If Apple refunds or reverses a purchase, we may remove the items or benefits you received from it to the extent the game is able to.

Consumers in the EU and the UK: right of withdrawal for digital content

If you live in the EU or the UK and you are a consumer, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a purchase made at a distance without giving a reason. Digital content that is supplied straight away is an exception. You lose the right to withdraw once delivery has begun, if you have expressly agreed to immediate delivery and have acknowledged that you will lose the right.

All our products are digital content or digital services that the game delivers immediately when you complete your purchase. By confirming a purchase with Apple, you ask us to start delivery immediately, and you acknowledge that once delivery has begun you lose your right to withdraw from that purchase. For Chief's Club, the service also starts immediately. If you withdraw from a subscription within the withdrawal period, you may need to pay a proportionate amount for the service you have already received. Requests to withdraw, and refund requests, go through Apple as described above. Apple's own terms may also give you rights.

This notice does not affect your legal rights if digital content is faulty or not as described. Other countries have their own consumer laws and nothing in these terms takes those rights away.

7. Randomized crates

Some crates in the game contain randomized rewards. This section is a summary, and the full odds and reward sizes are on the Crate Odds and Rewards page.

  • Random rewards. When you open a crate, the game draws one reward at random from the odds for that crate type. The reward can be cash, Gold Badges or a piece of gear. Nothing can be guaranteed except the pity rule below.
  • The odds are published. The odds are on the Crate Odds and Rewards page at {{ODDS_URL}}, and they are shown inside the game before you buy. They are the actual odds the game uses.
  • Pity rule. Opening elite or legend crates guarantees at least one Epic or Legendary gear item within every 10 opens. Standard crates do not count toward it.
  • Crates can be earned for free. You can earn crates through jobs, bounties, daily rewards, the Career Pass and the free Supply Drop, which gives one standard crate every 6 hours.
  • Buying crates. You buy crates with Gold Badges, not directly with money. Gold Badges can themselves be bought with real money, so buying crates can involve real money in that way. The Rookie Starter Pack and Daily Deal A also include crates.
  • Belgium. The game turns off buying crates with Gold Badges when your device's region is set to Belgium. This affects only buying crates with Gold Badges.
  • Crate rewards have no cash value, and you cannot sell or exchange them (see section 3).

8. Spending controls

You are in charge of what is spent on your Apple Account. Apple gives you these controls. They work on the device and Apple Account and do not need anything from us.

  • Screen Time. On the device, go to Settings, then Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions. In the App Store purchases section, set In-App Purchases to Don't Allow to block them. You can also require a password for every purchase, and protect your Screen Time settings with a passcode so they cannot be changed.
  • Ask to Buy. If you use Family Sharing, the family organizer can turn on Ask to Buy for a child's account. Each purchase request is then sent to a parent or guardian to approve or decline before it goes through.
  • Family Sharing of purchases. We have not turned on Family Sharing for the purchases in this game, so the things you buy are for the Apple Account that bought them. Consumables cannot be shared in any case.
  • Password and Face ID. Apple's own purchase settings let you require your Apple Account password or Face ID or Touch ID for purchases.
  • Check what you have spent. Apple emails you a receipt for each purchase, and your Apple Account keeps your purchase history.

The game is for people who are old enough to make purchases, or who have a parent's or guardian's permission (see the Terms of Use).
