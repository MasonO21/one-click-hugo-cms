Crate Odds and Rewards

Last updated {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · Applies to {{APP_NAME}} version {{VERSION}}

The short version

  • Every crate opening gives you exactly one reward, chosen at random: cash, Gold Badges, or a piece of gear.
  • The percentages on this page are the actual odds the game uses.
  • There are three crate tiers: Standard, Elite and Legend. Higher tiers have better odds.
  • Opening Elite or Legend crates guarantees at least one Epic or Legendary gear item within every 10 opens.
  • You can earn crates for free. You can also buy crates with Gold Badges. The game is not offered in Belgium or Brazil (see section 6).

1. Crate types and prices

There are three crate tiers. In each of the three worlds (Night Precinct, Ember Station and Golden Hour) a tier has its own name, but the odds for a tier are the same in every world.

Put another way, the chance that one opening gives an Epic or Legendary gear item is 0% for a Standard crate, 20% (17% plus 3%) for an Elite crate and 50% (35% plus 15%) for a Legend crate. This is before the pity guarantee in section 5.

A cash reward is about the amount of income shown in the table, multiplied by a random factor between 0.8 and 1.4. So a Standard crate gives cash worth about 8 to 14 minutes of income, an Elite crate about 48 to 84 minutes, and a Legend crate about 3.2 to 5.6 hours. A Gold Badges reward is a random amount in the range shown.

4. Gear bonuses and levels

Level cap. Each gear item can reach level 10 at most.

Duplicates. If a crate gives you a duplicate of a gear item that is already at level 10, the duplicate pays 3 Gold Badges.

5. The pity guarantee

Opening Elite or Legend crates guarantees at least one Epic or Legendary gear item within every 10 opens. In plain terms:

  • The game counts your Elite and Legend crate openings together. Standard crates are not counted.
  • If you open 9 Elite or Legend crates in a row and none of them gives Epic or Legendary gear, your 10th opening is guaranteed to give an Epic or Legendary gear item.
  • Whenever you receive Epic or Legendary gear, whether by luck or through the guarantee, the count starts again from zero.

Worked example

You have just started counting, and you open Elite crates one after another.

  1. Openings 1 to 9 give you cash, Gold Badges and Rare gear, but no Epic or Legendary gear. With the Elite odds, each opening has an 80% chance of not being Epic or Legendary (100% minus 20%), so a run of 9 like this happens about 13 times in 100.
  2. Opening 10 is now guaranteed to give an Epic or Legendary gear item, even though the normal chance for that opening is only 20%.
  3. The count starts again from zero, so you begin a new run of up to 10 openings.

Had you received Epic gear on opening 4 by luck, the count would have started again from zero at that point, so the guarantee would then apply no later than opening 14.

6. Free crates, Belgium and Brazil

  • Free crates. You can earn crates for free through jobs, bounties, daily rewards, the Career Pass and the Supply Drop, which gives one free Standard crate every 6 hours. Buying crates is optional.
  • Belgium and Brazil. The game is not offered on the App Store in Belgium or Brazil. As an extra safeguard, if your App Store country or your device's region is Belgium or Brazil, the game switches off paid random rewards: you cannot buy crates with Gold Badges, the Crate Trio deal is not offered, the Rookie Starter Pack gives 90 Gold Badges instead of its 3 crates, and the Career Pass tiers that would give crates (on the free and the premium track) give a fixed number of Gold Badges instead.

7. These are the real odds

The percentages, reward sizes and gear bonuses on this page are the actual ones the game uses. They are built into the game and fixed for each crate type. The game runs entirely on your device, so each draw is made on your device by a software random number generator. We do not see your results and we cannot change them. Apart from the pity guarantee above, each opening is independent of the ones before it: opening a crate does not make the next one more or less likely to be good.

If we change the odds, we will update this page and the odds shown in the game in the same release, and change the date at the top of this page. A change applies to crates opened after the update. The odds are also shown inside the game before you buy, and this page is published at {{ODDS_URL}}.
