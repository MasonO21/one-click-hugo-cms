# App Store Review Guidelines: how this app complies

Source of truth for what the app does: `docs/FACTS.md` and `docs/NATIVE_BRIDGE.md`, checked against `game/src`, `tools/` and `ios/`. Differences that were found and not fixed are listed in `README.md` under "Open points in the build".

**Read this first.** The guideline numbers below are from my memory of the App Store Review Guidelines. Apple renumbers and rewrites them. Top-level sections (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) are stable; sub-numbers are not. **Verify in App Store Connect** and in the current guidelines (developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/): **verify the exact guideline number** for every row before you quote it to App Review. I do not quote the guideline text; I describe the requirement in my own words.

"Status" means: **OK by design** = follows from `docs/FACTS.md` and the code; **Verified** = checked in the game source (and in a desktop browser with a mock bridge); **Check in build** = can only be confirmed in a running iOS build (device, Simulator, TestFlight or sandbox), which has not been done yet.

## Known review risks and mitigations

1. **Weekly subscription scrutiny.** Weekly auto-renewing subscriptions at 4.99 are examined closely: the price and the renewal terms must be unmistakable, and the subscription must give ongoing value.
   - Mitigation: the purchase sheet shows the title, "per week", the billed price as the largest text, what is included, renewal and cancel wording, and Terms, Privacy and Purchase Terms links; the Store card repeats the renewal line; Restore Purchases sits on the same tab (see `IAP.md`). No trial, no misleading "free" wording. Benefits are real and continuing (income x1.5, offline earnings, extra job slot, auto-collect, 25 Gold Badges per day). The description repeats the terms. If Apple asks, say the benefits are delivered on every device from the StoreKit entitlement.
   - Verified in the code: the game stores the subscription's expiry date, so benefits work in Airplane Mode and stop on their own when that date passes; a refunded subscription stops at the next launch or Restore Purchases. Offline earnings are worked out only after Chief's Club has been re-checked with the App Store (at most 3 seconds' wait), so a renewal that happened while the player was away pays at 100%. If Chief's Club lapses while a job is running in its extra slot, the job stays visible and can still be collected. Check in build: cancellation, expiry and Restore Purchases on a device.

2. **Web-view based app (Guideline 4.2).** The game is HTML and JavaScript in a `WKWebView`. Reviewers reject thin wrappers around a website.
   - Mitigation: content is bundled, works offline, not a site. Native functionality: StoreKit 2, save-file mirroring, haptics, safe-area layout, Safari view, iPad support. This is stated in `review_notes.txt` under "NATIVE FUNCTIONALITY".
   - If rejected under 4.2: reply in Resolution Center with the same list, a short screen recording of a purchase, Restore Purchases and Airplane Mode play, and ask which additional native feature they would like to see. Do not argue; add a genuinely native feature if needed (this is a code change, not part of this package).

3. **Sale of virtual currency with randomized items.** Gold Badges bought with real money buy random crates.
   - Mitigation: odds on every purchase that includes crates (the crate confirmation sheet, and the Crate Trio, Starter Pack and Career Pass Premium purchase sheets), in the Cases tab ("Drop rates"), in Settings and at `{{ODDS_URL}}`; pity rule stated; an approximate real-money value next to Gold Badge prices; "Contains loot boxes" as the first line of the description; free ways to get crates and Gold Badges (Rally Boost, Supply Drop, Daily Roll Call, jobs, bounties, Career Pass free track); no cash value; not offered in Belgium or Brazil, with an in-game safety net; loot-box answer "Yes" in the age rating (9+, 16+ in Australia).

4. **Name similarity.** The name must not resemble another app, or a trademark.
   - Mitigation: run the trademark and App Store search first (`EXPORT_COMPLIANCE_AND_RIGHTS.md` section 3). If ASC says the name is taken, change `APP_NAME` and re-run `python3 tools/apply_config.py`. Reserve the name early by creating the app record.

5. **Police, fire and EMS imagery and the fictional disclaimer.** Reviewers dislike apps that could pass for an official agency, and depicting emergency services can raise questions.
   - Mitigation: cartoon style, no real logos, badges, liveries or agency names, no red cross, red crescent or Star of Life (the EMS art uses a heartbeat mark and a heart emblem; do not add protected emblems), fictional disclaimer in the description, terms and inside the game (Credits). Screenshots show only the invented art.
   - Verified in the art code and the generated EMS screenshot: no red cross, no plus-shaped mark anywhere.

6. **Price display.** Prices that do not match what Apple charges are a common rejection reason.
   - Mitigation: the Store tab, the Career tab and the purchase sheets show StoreKit `displayPrice`, and the approximate money values next to Gold Badge prices are computed from StoreKit's price of the smallest pack (they are not shown until prices have loaded). Fixed price text that exists: "4.99 USD per week" in the description (keep it aligned with the price you set) and the US dollar reference values on the Precinct Patron card (labelled as such; they also drive the Patron perk). Do not put prices in screenshots or the name or subtitle (the generated screenshots show none).

7. **Restore Purchases presence.** Apps with non-consumables or subscriptions must offer a way to restore.
   - Mitigation: Restore Purchases in Settings, at the bottom of the Store tab and on the Career tab (the bridge has `restore`). The review notes say where it is. Verified in the code: the button exists, and offline it shows "Could not reach the App Store. Try again later.". Check in build that it works on a second install. Consumables are not restorable, which the Store footer ("Restore brings back one-time unlocks and Chief's Club; consumables such as Gold Badge packs cannot be restored."), the review notes and the Purchase Terms state.

8. **Products the reviewer cannot reach.** The Starter Pack is shown only for 48 hours after first launch (always present on a fresh install); the Auto-Clicker upgrade appears once the Auto-Clicker is owned.
   - Mitigation: all three Daily Deals are listed every day and the Evidence Safe can be bought at any time (`game/tests/native-mock.js` checks that every product is reachable on a new save). See "Reachability" in `IAP.md`. Still test on a fresh install before submitting.

9. **Variable-value consumable (Evidence Safe).** 2.99 for between 20 and 500 Gold Badges.
   - Mitigation: the Store card shows "Holds N Gold Badges (20 to 500)" and the purchase sheet shows "Evidence Safe: N Gold Badges" before buying. Verified in the game source.

10. **Progress lives on the device only.** Deleting the app without a backup deletes progress, including purchased Gold Badges (consumables cannot be restored by Apple).
    - Mitigation: stated in the description ("saved on your device only, with no cloud sync") and in the legal texts (check with the attorney that the Terms and Purchase Terms say it). Be ready to answer support emails about it.

11. **Spending-linked permanent perks (Precinct Patron).** Permanent income bonuses at spend thresholds.
    - Mitigation: disclosed in the description and review notes. The Career tab shows the thresholds and the player's lifetime spend (verified). They are dollar amounts based on the intended USD values, in every currency. A refunded purchase lowers the total again.

12. **Kids appeal.** The theme may attract children while the app is not a Kids app.
    - Mitigation: neutral metadata, no Kids category, honest age rating, no ads, no tracking, no personal data. See `AGE_RATING.md`.

13. **First-time issues on the tools side.** The Swift shell has never been compiled (`ios/README.md`) and the StoreKit code has not been run against Apple's sandbox (`SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`, last section). Crashes and hangs on launch are the most common reason for rejection under 2.1. Test on a real device, on the oldest supported iOS (16.0) and on iPad, before uploading.

14. **Timers, the device clock and paid shortcuts.** Reviewers and players dislike games that punish them for things outside their control and then sell the fix (streak restore for 15 Gold Badges, Rush with Gold Badges).
    - Verified in the code and by `game/tests/regressions.js`: moving the device clock back, or changing time zone, no longer breaks the Daily Roll Call streak (a later stored date counts as claimed, and a claim less than 48 hours ago keeps the streak even when a calendar day was skipped) and no longer locks the Rally Boost, Supply Drop, boosts or job timers (stored timestamps move back by the same amount). A promotion pays out jobs that have finished (running jobs keep running), and a job left in a Chief's Club slot that has lapsed can still be collected. Check in build: change the time zone and the clock on a device (test matrix).
