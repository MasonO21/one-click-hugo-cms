NOTES FOR APP REVIEW - {{APP_NAME}} {{VERSION}} (build {{BUILD}})

SIGN-IN: none needed. The app has no accounts and collects no personal data.

OFFLINE, NO TRACKING
Every feature works with no network (try Airplane Mode). The game makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity is Apple's StoreKit traffic and the legal and support links, which open in an in-app Safari view (SFSafariViewController). There is no general web browsing. No ads, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs; no IDFA, ATT prompt or permissions. App Privacy: Data Not Collected. Progress is saved on the device only: no server, no cloud sync.

NATIVE FUNCTIONALITY (not a repackaged website)
The game is bundled in the app and runs from local files in a WKWebView; it loads no remote code or content. The native shell adds StoreKit 2 purchases and Restore Purchases, save-file mirroring, haptics, safe-area layout and the Safari view.

WHERE TO FIND THINGS
- Store: STORE in the bottom tab bar (HQ, Roster, Gear-Up, Cases, Store, Career), or tap the Gold Badge counter at the top. Every purchase is listed there with its real StoreKit price. At the bottom: Restore Purchases, Manage Subscription, Settings and the legal links (Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Purchase Terms, Crate odds).
- Settings: HQ tab, "Settings and Legal" card, OPEN (or the Settings button in the Store tab): sound, haptics and Reduce flashing switches, Restore Purchases, Manage Subscription, legal links, Credits, Erase save.

IN-APP PURCHASES (Apple IAP; test with the sandbox account)
- Consumables: six Gold Badge packs, Evidence Safe, three Daily Deals (Crate Trio, Cash Crate, Recruit Rush).
- Non-consumables: Rookie Starter Pack, Career Pass Premium (Season 1), Auto-Clicker, Combo Auto-Clicker, and the upgrade between them.
- Auto-renewable subscription: Chief's Club (weekly).
To test: Store tab, tap a price, tap Buy, confirm in the sandbox sheet. Goods are granted once. Restore Purchases brings back non-consumables and Chief's Club (consumables are not restorable). If Apple refunds a one-time unlock, the game removes it.
Fresh install: the Starter Pack is offered for 48 hours after first launch; all three Daily Deals are listed every day (each can be bought once a day); the Evidence Safe always holds at least 20 Gold Badges; the Auto-Clicker and Combo Auto-Clicker are both offered, and the upgrade appears after the Auto-Clicker is bought.
Gold Badges have no cash value and cannot be transferred or exchanged. Purchases also raise a lifetime "Precinct Patron" rank (permanent income bonus), calculated on the device.

RANDOMIZED CRATES
Crates give randomized rewards (cash, Gold Badges, gear). They can be earned free or bought with Gold Badges. Odds are shown before every purchase that includes crates (the crate purchase confirmation, and the Starter Pack, Crate Trio and Career Pass sheets), in the Cases tab ("Drop rates"), in Settings and at {{ODDS_URL}}. Gold Badge prices show an approximate real-money value. The app is not distributed in Belgium or Brazil; it also switches off paid crates if the App Store country or the device region is BE or BR.

SUBSCRIPTION: CHIEF'S CLUB
One-week auto-renewable subscription (group "Chief's Club") on a card in the Store tab. The purchase screen shows the price and "per week", the benefits, renewal and cancellation terms, and links to Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Purchase Terms. Benefits: all income x1.5, offline earnings at 100% (cap of at least 8 hours), +1 job slot, bounties auto-collect, 25 Gold Badges per day (Claim button on the card). No free trial. Cancel with Manage Subscription or in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

CONTENT
Cartoon style: no blood, no injuries, no weapons fired. Fiction, not affiliated with any real agency.
