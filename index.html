Night Precinct: legal and support

Documents effective September 29, 2026 · written for Night Precinct version 1.0.0

Night Precinct is an offline idle game for iPhone and iPad, published by Mason Osborn. These pages explain how the game treats your privacy, the rules for using it, what you can buy and how the randomized crates work, and where to get help.

In short

The game has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send any information about you to us or to anyone else. Your progress is stored on your own device. Purchases, if you choose to make any, are handled entirely by Apple.
